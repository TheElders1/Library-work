Library profile

Our vision, our mission and the university's publications.

Library vision

To be the intellectual heart of Madonna University—a dynamic center of discovery and learning that empowers the university community to achieve the highest standards of academic excellence while nurturing the spiritual and ethical formation of the total person.

Library mission

In alignment with Madonna University's dedication to defining the path of academic and spiritual growth, the Library commits to:

  1. 1

    Advancing Academic Excellence

    We provide comprehensive, authoritative, and accessible resources—both physical and digital—that support rigorous scholarship, research, and the curricula of the University.

  2. 2

    Fostering Total Person Formation

    We create an inclusive and welcoming environment that encourages intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning, supporting the development of the whole person.

  3. 3

    Promoting Integrity and Humility

    We uphold the highest standards of academic integrity and intellectual freedom. Through our services and collections, we model and encourage ethical scholarship, responsible citizenship, and a spirit of humble service to others.

  4. 4

    Serving with Compassion

    We provide user-centered services delivered by a dedicated staff who treat every student, faculty, and researcher with dignity and respect, ensuring equitable access to information for all.
